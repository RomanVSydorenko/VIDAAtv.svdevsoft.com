📋 Installation Instructions (VIDAA)

  1. Download: Click the download button above to get komitex-tv.wgt
  2. Copy to USB: Copy the .wgt file to a USB drive (root directory)
  3. Enable Developer Mode on TV:
    • Press Home on your remote
    • Go to Settings → System → About
    • Find "Software Version" and press OK 5 times quickly
    • Enter PIN: 0000 or 1234
  4. Install App:
    • Insert USB into your Hisense TV
    • Go to Home → Apps
    • Select "Install from USB" or "Developer"
    • Choose komitex-tv.wgt
  5. Launch: Find "Komitex TV" in your apps and enjoy!

🤖 Android/Google TV Installation (APK)

  1. Download APK: On your Android TV, open a browser or the "Downloader" app and go to:
    https://romanvsydorenko.github.io/VIDAAtv.svdevsoft.com/apk/net.komitex.newtv-Signed.apk
  2. Enable Unknown Sources: Settings → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → Allow for your browser/downloader.
  3. Install: Open the downloaded APK and confirm installation.
  4. Launch: Find Komitex TV in your apps.

Note: APK installs only on Android/Google TV devices. For Hisense VIDAA models use the .wgt package above.
